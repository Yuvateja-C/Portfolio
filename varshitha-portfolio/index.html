01

Glyphora — Building a Real Estate Marketplace from Zero

Product Strategy · Marketplace Design · Full-Stack Development

Executive Summary

Built a full-stack real estate marketplace from concept to live production as a solo founder while completing a Computer Science degree. The platform connects property buyers and sellers through an end-to-end digital experience — featuring property discovery, offer management, mortgage calculators, and subscription-based premium listings.

Problem Identified

Property buyers and sellers in emerging markets face fragmented listing services, lack of transparency in property details, and no unified digital platform for the full buyer journey — from search through evaluation, offer, and financing.

Target Users

  • Property Buyers: Individuals seeking transparent, verified property listings with tools to evaluate and compare
  • Property Sellers: Owners wanting digital reach and a structured platform to list, manage, and sell properties
  • Premium Subscribers: Users wanting highlighted visibility and priority listing placement

Product Decisions

  • Multi-sided marketplace: Designed for both buyer and seller workflows, requiring careful chicken-and-egg growth strategy
  • Subscription monetization: Revenue through premium listing tiers rather than per-transaction fees — reducing friction for initial adoption
  • Mortgage calculator: Added as a value-add retention feature — keeping buyers on-platform during the evaluation phase
  • Multilingual support (EN/AR): Expanded addressable market by supporting Arabic-speaking users
  • Glassmorphism dark UI: Premium visual positioning to differentiate from generic listing sites
  • Skeleton loading states: Perceived performance optimization for a better UX during data fetching

Technical Architecture

  • Laravel/PHP backend for rapid prototyping and MVC structure
  • Bootstrap 5 responsive frontend with custom glassmorphism design system
  • Google Maps API integration for interactive property search
  • Subscription billing system with tiered access
  • Wishlist/save functionality for buyer engagement
  • SEO-optimized property detail pages

Challenges & Trade-offs

  • Solo founder constraint: Wore all hats — PM, engineer, designer, and growth. Learned to ruthlessly prioritize features
  • Framework choice: Chose Laravel for speed-to-market over a potentially more scalable option like Next.js — acceptable for MVP validation
  • Buyer-first strategy: Prioritized buyer experience (search, filters, mortgage tools) before building out comprehensive seller tools — following the marketplace principle of solving one side's problem first

Lessons Learned

  • 0-to-1 product development requires comfort with ambiguity and rapid decision-making
  • Multi-sided marketplaces need careful sequencing of supply and demand features
  • International considerations (multilingual, currency, cultural norms) compound product complexity exponentially
  • The fastest way to learn product management is to ship a real product to real users
02

Speech Emotion Recognition — Evaluating AI Model Trade-offs

AI Research · Model Benchmarking · Product-Oriented Evaluation

Executive Summary

Comparative evaluation of classical machine learning (SVM) versus deep learning (Bidirectional LSTM RNN) for detecting human emotions from speech audio — a foundational problem in human-computer interaction, accessibility, and empathetic AI systems.

Problem Statement

Accurately detecting human emotions from speech is critical for accessibility tools, call center quality assurance, mental health monitoring, and building empathetic AI assistants. However, choosing between classical ML and deep learning approaches requires rigorous benchmarking across performance, complexity, and resource trade-offs — exactly the kind of evaluation an AI Product Manager must make.

Business Context & Target Users

  • Call center platforms: Automated sentiment detection for customer experience monitoring
  • Mental health tech: Continuous emotional state tracking for therapeutic applications
  • Accessibility tools: Emotion-aware assistive technologies for neurodiverse users
  • AI assistants: Building more empathetic and context-aware conversational AI

Methodology

  • Used the RAVDESS (Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song) corpus as the benchmark dataset
  • Implemented Support Vector Machine (SVM) as the classical ML baseline
  • Implemented Bidirectional LSTM RNN as the deep learning approach
  • Compared both methods on emotion classification accuracy and complexity

Product Thinking Applied

  • This is exactly how an AI PM evaluates model selection — comparing approaches on performance, computational cost, data requirements, and deployment complexity before making a build decision
  • Deep learning isn't always the right answer — trade-offs in latency, cost, and maintainability often make simpler models the better product choice
  • Web deployment of ML models (via GitHub Pages) bridges the gap between research and usable product

Lessons Learned

  • AI model selection is fundamentally a product decision, not purely a technical one
  • Benchmarking methodology matters as much as results — reproducibility and clear evaluation criteria are essential
  • The ability to communicate AI trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders is a critical PM skill
